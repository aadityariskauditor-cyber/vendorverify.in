01 / The Problem

Why MSMEs Lose Contracts

The compliance wall is real — and it's costing you orders

  • Large buyers now require ESG/BRSR documentation before vendor empanelment — and most MSMEs have none.

  • EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) will levy tariffs on your exports if you can't prove low-carbon credentials.

  • ZED certification unlocks government incentives and PSU contracts — yet only a fraction of eligible MSMEs have applied.

  • Generic consultants don't understand factory floors. Reports full of jargon, zero implementation support.

08 / FAQ

Common Questions

Straight answers, no jargon

Yes — and increasingly urgently so. Large manufacturers (Tata, Mahindra, Reliance, global exporters) now require ESG compliance from their entire supply chain. If you supply to any large Indian company or export to the EU/US, ESG documentation is already or soon will be mandatory. The ZED scheme from Ministry of MSME specifically targets small units with incentives that often exceed the cost of certification.

FLAGO™ stands for Financial, Legal, Audit, Governance, and Operational risk. It's a proprietary scoring framework I developed specifically for Indian MSME supplier assessment. It combines public data, audit findings, and ESG disclosures into a single credibility score (0–100) that international buyers can use to qualify Indian suppliers. Think of it as a CIBIL score for your ESG and compliance health. The full methodology is available on request to prospective clients and buyers.

ZED Bronze typically takes 4–6 months from initial audit to certification, assuming the MSME is reasonably cooperative and responsive to documentation requests. Silver and Gold add 2–4 months each. I've never had a client take longer than 8 months for Bronze when following my structured programme. Timeline depends significantly on how quickly you implement recommended process changes.

Yes. While I'm based in Sonipat, Haryana, I work with MSMEs across India. For clients in Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, and Gujarat, I typically conduct one on-site audit visit and manage the rest remotely via WhatsApp, video calls, and document sharing. Travel costs for on-site visits outside Haryana/Delhi NCR are charged at actuals.

BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting) is mandatory for SEBI-listed companies and increasingly requested by large Indian corporates from their suppliers. GRI (Global Reporting Initiative) is the international standard used by EU/US buyers and multinational corporations. For export-oriented MSMEs I recommend a combined approach: GRI-aligned content formatted to also satisfy BRSR requirements, so one report serves both domestic and international buyers.
